OUR PURPOSE

A living connection
between faith
and everyday life.

Islamic knowledge should be reflected in how we live, how we treat others, and how we serve our communities.

Kanzuli Mo'arif Islamic Order is a faith-centred institution founded by USTAZ TAUHEEB ONI .O.A. Our purpose brings together education, spiritual growth, good character, and service to humanity.

We welcome children, young people, and adults to grow in understanding and put their learning into practice.

Our vision +

To build a community where Islamic knowledge inspires upright character, meaningful lives, and compassionate service to humanity.

Our mission +

To make Islamic learning accessible, nurture spiritual and moral development, support people in need, and demonstrate Islamic values through education and community service.

TUITION & ENROLMENT

Clear fees.
Purposeful learning.

Our education programme is fee-paying. Registration is a one-time fee, separate from recurring tuition.

FeeOnline individual lessonsPhysical classes
One-time registration₦10,000₦3,000

UniformNot applicable₦15,000 · compulsory for all physical learners

Returning online learners pay ₦50,000 per term for tuition and e-books. Physical kitab costs are estimates and will be confirmed for the learner’s class. Confirm any subsequent book or replacement uniform requirements with the institution.

Payment arrangements

  1. Pay registration first. Registration is compulsory for online and physical learners and is paid once.
  2. Tuition may be paid in up to two instalments. Full tuition payment is due no later than the end of the second week after resumption.
  3. Online e-books must be paid for in full before classes begin each term. The ₦10,000 materials fee is separate from tuition instalments.
  4. All physical learners must purchase the ₦15,000 uniform. Kitabs are purchased according to the learner’s class.

What your tuition supports

Fees help fund teachers’ salaries, online subscriptions, management of our temporary learning venue, and savings towards a permanent institutional site.

Complete the student registration form first. Your acknowledgement, payment reference and bank details will be sent privately to your email after submission. Admission and class placement are confirmed after payment verification; no payment is taken on this website.

STUDENT ADMISSIONS

Ready to begin?
Register as a learner.

Complete your application through our official Google-hosted registration form. Learners under 18 must be registered by a parent or guardian.

  1. Complete and review the registration form. Email is compulsory. Tell us about the learner’s level and preferred class arrangement.
  2. Receive your acknowledgement by email. Your registration reference, applicable fees, private bank details and payment memo instructions are sent to the email you provide.
  3. Make payment and provide evidence. Reply to the acknowledgement email with your reference and payment evidence for verification.
  4. Receive your receipt and class confirmation. The institution confirms payment, admission, placement and lesson times.

Submitting an application does not confirm payment or admission. Registration is paid first; tuition may be split into up to two instalments and must be fully paid by the end of Week 2 after resumption.
